Product Valuation Check Report
------------------------------

Enterprise report (account.report framework) that verifies, per stock
valuation account, that the product valuation
(``deltatech_stock_valuation``) is consistent with the accounting
balance — and explains any difference.

Columns
~~~~~~~

- **Account Balance** — full balance of the account from posted journal
  items, up to the selected date
- **Valuation (lines with product)** — total of the journal items
  carrying a product, which is by definition what the product valuation
  aggregates
- **Difference (lines without product)** — journal items posted on stock
  accounts without a product; these cannot be allocated to any product
  valuation

Usage
~~~~~

A caret action on each account row opens the journal items without
product, so the difference can be audited and fixed directly (assign the
product or move the line to a non-valuation account).

Dependencies
~~~~~~~~~~~~

- ``account_reports`` (Odoo Enterprise)
- ``deltatech_stock_valuation``
