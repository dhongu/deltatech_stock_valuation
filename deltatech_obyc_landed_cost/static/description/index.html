Overview

Landed costs on top of deltatech_obyc. The stock account of a landed cost comes from the OBYC rule landed_cost; this module adds, per company, an optional routing of the class 6 credit through a technical intermediary account, and fills the landed cost created from a vendor bill.

Usage

Configuration

  1. Go to Inventory → Configuration → Settings, block Valuation.
  2. Set Landed Cost Class 6 Method to Through intermediary account and choose the Intermediary Account (a technical account defined by the company accounting policy, not a class 6 account).
  3. Make sure the OBYC rule Landed Costs (landed_cost) has the Valuation Account filled.
1 Landed cost method setting

Usage

  1. Confirm a purchase order for the goods.
2 Confirmed purchase order
  1. Validate the receipt of the purchase order. OBYC posts Dr stock / Cr goods received not invoiced.
3 Validated receipt
4 Receipt entry
  1. Create and post the vendor bill of the goods.

  1. Post the transport vendor bill, with a line of a product flagged as landed cost, then click Create Landed Costs. The receipts of the purchase orders on the bill not yet covered by another landed cost are filled in (otherwise add the receipt in the Transfers field), and the account of each cost line is taken from the bill line.
6 Transport bill with the Create Landed Costs button
  1. Compute and Validate the landed cost.
7 Validated landed cost
  1. With the standard method the entry is Dr stock / Cr 624:

With the intermediary method it is Dr stock / Cr intermediary and Dr intermediary / Cr 624:

Class 609, accounts outside class 6 and the debit of a negative cost are never rerouted.

Versions

19.0.1.0.1 (2026-11-11)

  • Consultant sheet and USAGE rewritten as the full process: purchase order, receipt, goods vendor bill, transport vendor bill, landed cost and its entry; the setting moved to the configuration section. New screenshots, no functional change.

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-11-11)

  • First version. Replaces l10n_ro_stock_account_landed_cost_account for companies that use deltatech_obyc instead of l10n_ro_stock_account: the stock account comes from the OBYC rule landed_cost (no more account on the location). A landed cost entry that would credit a class 6 account (except 609) can be routed through an intermediary account (Dr stock / Cr intermediary and Dr intermediary / Cr class 6), configured per company in Inventory settings. The landed cost created from a vendor bill takes the receipts of the bill's purchase orders not yet covered by another landed cost and the account of each bill line.
