Moves stock from one account to another with a document of its own: a
rule says which pairs of accounts are allowed, the document records the
source and destination locations and the products, and its validation
creates the stock transfer and posts the journal entry between the two
accounts at the cost of the goods.

The document keeps the history (who, when, what, from which account to
which), links the transfer and the journal entry, and can be reversed,
with a reverse transfer and a reversal entry. Access is limited to a
dedicated group.

Usage
=====

1. Define the allowed pairs of accounts
---------------------------------------

Open **Inventory → Operations → Reclassification Rules** and create a
rule for each reclassification you accept: the **Source Account**, the
**Destination Account** and, optionally:

- **Management Decision Required**: the document cannot be validated
  without the text of the management decision (use it when the
  destination of the goods changes);
- **Reason Required**: the document cannot be validated without a
  reason.

The pair of accounts is unique per company and the two accounts must
differ. No rule is delivered with the module: each company creates the
pairs it needs (for example *3028 → 371*).

|Reclassification rule 302800 → 371000: Source Account 302800 Other
consumables and Destination Account 371000 Merchandise are highlighted,
Destination Type is Stock account change, Management Decision Required
is ticked and Reason Required is not|

2. Create the reclassification document
---------------------------------------

Open **Inventory → Operations → Reclassifications** and create a
document:

- **Rule**: brings the source and destination accounts (read only);
- **Source Location** and **Destination Location**: they can be the same
  location, when only the account changes, or two locations; the
  destination can also be a location outside the stock, but only when
  the destination account is not a stock account (for example stock →
  fixed asset): with a stock-to-stock rule the goods would leave the
  stock while the destination account keeps the value;
- **Journal** and **Date**, and **Management Decision** / **Reason**
  when the rule asks for them;
- in the **Products** tab: the product, the **Lot/Serial Number**
  (required for tracked products; a serial number has quantity 1), the
  **Quantity** and, optionally, the **Vendor Bill Line** the goods were
  bought with. The quantity reclassified on a bill line cannot exceed
  the quantity of the line.

|Draft reclassification: rule 302800 → 371000 and the Validate button
are highlighted; source and destination location DC/Stock, journal
Inventory Valuation, management decision filled in and the Products tab
with Marfă de reclasificat, quantity 4.00|

3. Validate
-----------

Press **Validate**. The module checks the rule, the decision and the
reason, the lots and serial numbers and the stock available in the
source location, then:

1. does an internal transfer of the products (it does not post the
   automatic valuation entry);
2. sets the **Unit Cost** and the **Value** of each line: the cost of
   the goods in stock at the validation date;
3. posts the journal entry on the chosen journal.

The document moves to **Done** and links the **Transfer** and the
**Journal Entry**.

|Validated reclassification: the status is Done, the Transfer
(DC/INT/00001) and Journal Entry fields are highlighted, and the product
line shows quantity 4.00, unit cost 25.00 lei and value 100.00 lei|

4. The journal entry
--------------------

Open the **Journal Entry** from the document: it is posted on the chosen
journal, with the reference of the document. It debits the destination
account and credits the source account at the cost of the goods.

|Journal entry of the reclassification: credit 302800 Other consumables
100.00 lei and debit 371000 Merchandise 100.00 lei, with the product in
the label of both lines|

5. Decision or reason required by the rule
------------------------------------------

When the rule asks for the **Management Decision** (or the **Reason**),
the field is required on the document. If it is empty, **Validate** does
not go through: the field turns red and the message *Missing required
fields* appears. Fill in the field and validate again.

|Draft document without the management decision after pressing Validate:
the Management Decision field is red and the message Missing required
fields appears in the top right corner; the document stays in Draft|

6. Reverse
----------

**Reverse** on a validated document does a reverse transfer (from the
destination location back to the source one, with the same products and
serial numbers) and posts a reversal entry: the same amounts with the
accounts swapped (Dr source account / Cr destination account), dated
today, in positive amounts. If the stock in the destination is no longer
enough, the reversal is refused. A draft document has no transfer or
entry: delete it. A validated document cannot be deleted, only reversed.
Reverse it only from the document, not with *Reverse Entry* on the
journal entry: that button does not reverse the transfer, so the
document and the entry would no longer match.

|Reversed document: the status is Reversed and the Reverse Transfer and
Reversal Entry fields are highlighted next to the Transfer and Journal
Entry|

7. Stock reports
----------------

With the *Analytic Stock Balance and Stock Sheet* module
(``l10n_ro_stock_sheet``) installed, open **Inventory → Reporting →
Balanță analitică stocuri** and unfold the account and the product: the
reclassification shows as an **outgoing** line on the source account and
an **incoming** line on the destination account, with the quantity and
the value of the line, on the row of the transfer; the reversal shows as
the opposite lines. The difference between the analytic and the
synthetic balance does not change. With a destination outside the stock,
only the outgoing line from the source account appears (the fixed asset
accounts are not stock accounts).

|Analytic stock balance unfolded by account, product and document after
a reclassification and a reversal: outgoing lines on the source account,
incoming lines on the destination account|

Access
------

Two groups control the module: **Reclassifications** (create and
validate documents) and **Reclassification Configuration** (manage the
rules). The **Reclassification Rules** menu (under **Inventory →
Operations**) is visible only to the second one.

Changelog
=========

19.0.1.0.2 (2026-11-12)
-----------------------

- The moves of the transfer (and of the reversal transfer on cancel) are
  linked to the journal entry of the document, and a transfer between
  two internal locations gets the value of the line on its moves.
  Without this the stock reports (analytic stock balance, stock sheet)
  did not show the reclassification: they take the stock account and the
  value of a move from its entry, so the analytic balance stayed on the
  source account while the entry moved the value.
- "Cancel" of a validated document is now **Reverse** (state
  **Reversed**): the reversal entry has the same amounts with the
  accounts swapped, in positive amounts, instead of a negative "storno"
  entry, because the stock reports read the accounts of a
  reclassification from the debit and the credit of the entry. The
  reversal entry is posted with elevated rights, so the operator who
  validated the document can also reverse it without accounting rights.
  A draft document has no Cancel button any more: it is deleted, so the
  state Reversed always means a document that had a transfer and an
  entry.
- With ``l10n_ro_stock_gestiune`` installed, the entry of a document
  with one line is also set as the inter-warehouse transfer entry of its
  move, so that the stock sheet per warehouse or location shows the
  source account on the source and the destination account on the
  destination (it needs ``l10n_ro_stock_gestiune`` 19.0.3.1.8 or newer,
  which does not post its own transfer entry for these moves). The sheet
  pairs the entry with the move without looking at the product, so a
  document with several lines does not write it: its entry would be
  repeated for every move.
- The reverse transfer uses the operation type of the original transfer
  (it took the first internal type found, for example Quality Control,
  when the destination of the document was a location outside the
  warehouses), and its move gets the value of the document, so that the
  stock reports match the reversal entry even if the cost of the product
  changed meanwhile.
- The context key that stops the automatic valuation entry is renamed
  ``skip_stock_valuation_entry`` (it needs ``deltatech_obyc``
  19.0.1.0.23 or newer when both are installed).

19.0.1.0.1 (2026-11-11)
-----------------------

- Fix: a document with two lines of the same product could not be
  validated: the transfer merged their moves on confirmation and the
  lines no longer matched the moves. The moves are now confirmed without
  merge.
- The accounts of a rule must belong to the company of the rule.

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-11-11)
-----------------------

- First version: reclassification rules by pair of accounts, the
  reclassification document with products (lot/serial required for
  tracked products, vendor bill line optional), validation with transfer
  and journal entry, cancellation with reversal, dedicated groups.
