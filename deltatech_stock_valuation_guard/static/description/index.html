Overview

Two checks that keep the stock valuation trustworthy, each switched on per company.

One cost method for every product category

In Odoo the cost method (Standard, FIFO, Average) and the valuation (periodic or perpetual) are set on each product category. Nothing stops two categories of the same company from using different methods, and over the years a database ends up with a mix — some categories on FIFO, others on Standard — which the accounting has to explain at every closing.

  • With One Cost Method ticked, a product category can only use the cost method and the valuation of the company: creating or changing a category to another method is refused.
  • Align the categories puts every category of the company on its method in one step. Odoo recomputes the cost of the products of every category whose method changes, so the current costs are saved first, in a JSON attachment on the company, to allow a targeted revert.

No stock move without a value

A product that enters the stock at zero value — an opening inventory counted without costs, a quantity typed on the product form, a receipt at price zero — leaves the stock at zero value too, and the cost of goods sold of every later sale is zero. With Block Unvalued Moves ticked:

  • an incoming move of a storable product is refused when its value would be zero: receipt without a price, inventory gain or quantity update of a product without cost, return valued at zero;
  • an outgoing move is refused when the product has no cost.

Control lists (Inventory > Reporting > Valuation Control)

  • ✓
    categories using another cost method than the company;
  • ✓
    storable products without cost;
  • ✓
    stock valued at zero: incoming moves valued at zero with quantity still left in stock, that the next sales will take out at zero cost;
  • ✓
    moves valued at zero in the last 30 days.

The module blocks the moves validated by users. Moves validated by a process the user does not drive, such as the orders of the Point of Sale, are handled by Stock Valuation Guard - Point of Sale, which stops the product at the cart instead.

Configuration

  1. Go to Inventory > Configuration > Settings, section Valuation.
  2. Tick One Cost Method, check the Cost Method and Valuation of the company below it, and save.
  3. Click Align the categories to put every product category on the method of the company. Do it first on a test database: the cost of the products of the changed categories is recomputed. The previous costs are saved in an attachment on the company.
  4. Tick Block Unvalued Moves to refuse the stock moves that would carry no value.

Usage

  1. Before switching the blocking on, open Inventory > Reporting > Valuation Control > Storable Products without Cost and set the cost of the products you still sell.
  2. With Block Unvalued Moves on, validating a receipt, a delivery or an inventory adjustment of a product without value shows the list of the products to fix. Set the cost of the product, or the price on the purchase order, and validate again.
  3. Stock Valued at Zero lists the stock that entered at zero value and is still on hand. Its revaluation is an accounting decision (which cost, which entry); the list shows what it concerns.
  4. Moves Valued at Zero shows the receipts and deliveries of the last 30 days that were valued at zero, to follow the effect of the checks.
