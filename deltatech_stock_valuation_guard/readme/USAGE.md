1. Before switching the blocking on, open *Inventory > Reporting > Valuation Control >
   Storable Products without Cost* and set the cost of the products you still sell.
2. With *Block Unvalued Moves* on, validating a receipt, a delivery or an inventory
   adjustment of a product without value shows the list of the products to fix. Set the
   cost of the product, or the price on the purchase order, and validate again.
3. *Stock Valued at Zero* lists the stock that entered at zero value and is still on hand.
   Its revaluation is an accounting decision (which cost, which entry); the list shows
   what it concerns.
4. *Moves Valued at Zero* shows the receipts and deliveries of the last 30 days that were
   valued at zero, to follow the effect of the checks.
