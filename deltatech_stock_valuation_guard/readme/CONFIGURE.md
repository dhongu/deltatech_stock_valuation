1. Go to *Inventory > Configuration > Settings*, section *Valuation*.
2. Tick *One Cost Method*, check the *Cost Method* and *Valuation* of the company below it,
   and save.
3. Click *Align the categories* to put every product category on the method of the
   company. Do it first on a test database: the cost of the products of the changed
   categories is recomputed. The previous costs are saved in an attachment on the company.
4. Tick *Block Unvalued Moves* to refuse the stock moves that would carry no value.
